⚙️ Flywheel Overspeed Burst

A steel flywheel — hub, spokes, rim — spinning flat in zero gravity. Its own rotation is the only load: the solver's centrifugal term presses every bond inward, hardest at the rim where ω²r is largest. Below the burst speed it just hums. Raise the RPM (or press Spin up to burst) and the rim cracks and flings off arcs, stage by stage, leaving the hub and spokes spinning inside a cloud of their own debris. Turn Centrifugal off and it spins forever intact — even at redline. Drop the Bond strength to burst it apart completely. Strength and shape apply live.
