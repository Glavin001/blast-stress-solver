🏠 Destructible House

A realistic, low-poly, walkable wood-framed house built as one heterogeneous body with a real structural load path: a post-and-beam frame (corner + wall posts, interior king posts, a top-plate ring beam and a ridge beam) carries the pitched roof down to a static foundation. The drywall is non-structural infill, and the furniture barely rests on the floor. So knocking out drywall doesn't bring the house down — but take out the posts/beams under the roof and that bay collapses. Press V to walk inside (WASD + mouse); click to throw a ball.
