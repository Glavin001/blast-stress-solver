🧱 Keystone

A dry-stone arch — no mortar, no bolts. Every block is held aloft by nothing but the compression passing from stone to stone down to the ground. The coloured lines are the live per-bond stress from the solver; the default Compression view reveals the arch's thrust line. The gold block at the crown is the keystone: knock it out and the ring loses what locks it, so the two half-arches peel off their abutments and fall. Or crank the Load until the haunches hinge, drop a weight on a haunch, or click to shoot a stone out.
