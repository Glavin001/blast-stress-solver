🌀 Centrifugal Spinner

A free-floating beam spun in zero gravity. With Centrifugal off it spins forever, intact. Turn it on and the inward (centripetal) load fights the bonds: at the default high Bond strength the beam still holds, so lower Bond strength (then Reset) or raise the Spin rate until the load wins and it snaps apart. That crossover is the stress solver's centrifugal acceleration at work.
