🌀 Centrifugal Spinner

A free-floating beam spun in zero gravity. With Centrifugal off it spins forever, intact. Turn it on and the inward (centripetal) load loads the bonds in compression — strongest at the centre — so at the default settings a few bonds near the middle give way while the ends hang on. Raise Bond strength for fewer breaks (it goes fully intact past the threshold), lower it for more, or change the Spin rate (stress grows with spin²). Strength and shape apply live.
