A concrete cantilever built into a wall, loaded near its breaking point: the root is deep red — bonds have crossed the elastic limit (cracking) but haven't ruptured. Nudge Load up to snap it at the fatal limit (the beam falls), or down to relieve the cracks. The failure is tension at the top fiber — switch the Stress view to Tension vs Compression to see it.
