🧹 Stress Cracks

A concrete cantilever built into a wall. Drag Load up and watch stress concentrate at the root: bonds bloom green→yellow→red as they cross the elastic limit (cracking), then rupture at the fatal limit and the beam falls. The failure is tension at the top fiber — switch the Stress view to Tension vs Compression to see it. The colored lines are live per-bond stress straight from the solver. Click to throw a small ball, too.
