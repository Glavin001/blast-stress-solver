blast-stress-solver

Interactive physics-destruction demos for the NVIDIA Blast stress solver — real-time fracture with Rapier & Three.js, WASM under the hood.

🖱️ Drag to orbit · click to shoot 🎮 V first-person · WASD walk · Space jetpack ⚙️ Open the panel for weapons, physics & a live frame profiler
