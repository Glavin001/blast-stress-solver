blast-stress-solver

Interactive demos showcasing the NVIDIA Blast stress solver runtime — physics-based destruction with Rapier & Three.js. Every destruction demo carries a live ⚡ frame-profiler overlay: a real-time per-phase breakdown of what each frame costs (and what causes a dip below 60 fps), with an A/B toggle for the old split planner.

🎮 Every destruction demo now has a first-person shooter mode — toggle it in the right-hand panel, then WASD to walk, mouse/arrows to look, Space to jump, with a camera headlamp so you always see where you look. Left-click to shoot; press 1/2/3 (or Q) to switch between a ball projectile, a sticky-explosive launcher (stick charges to pillars, then F to detonate them all) and a grenade that explodes on contact. Shooting works in orbit mode too.

Split-Planner Benchmark
